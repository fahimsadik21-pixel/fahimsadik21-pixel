### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an applied **AI/ML Researcher** and **Full-Stack Software Engineer** currently in my final undergraduate semester (8th Semester) of **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering** at **Daffodil International University (DIU)**, maintaining a CGPA of **3.60 / 4.00**.

My core work lies at the intersection of **Healthcare AI**, **Explainable Machine Learning (XAI)**, and **Production Intelligent Systems**. I specialize in engineering leakage-proof, clinically reliable predictive pipelines and deploying them into scalable, clinician-oriented full-stack web platforms and IoT solutions.

- 🎓 **Academic Standing:** 8th Semester CSE, Daffodil International University (CGPA 3.60/4.00)
- 📍 **Location:** Savar, Dhaka, Bangladesh
- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Medical Image Diagnostics, Brain Stroke Detection, Clinical Survival Analysis, Multilingual NLP
- 💻 **Core Technical Arsenal:** Python, FastAPI, PyTorch, LightGBM, React, TypeScript, MySQL, ESP32
